Design a new SITE SUMMARY screen for SEO Audit Console at 1440px, DESKTOP, in the imported design system, LIGHT theme on cool paper (#F6F7F9 canvas, white panels, #E3E7EC hairlines, #10141A ink, #0F8F66 accent). This is a network/crawl + search-analytics tool with an analytics visual vibe: simple but powerful, the way serious SEO suites and AI-visibility challengers (Profound-style) present the state of a site on one page. The reader may be a business owner or managing director opening the dashboard cold, not only an SEO: the page must answer five questions top to bottom - how is the site doing, is it getting better or worse, what is broken, what to do first, how fresh is this data.

Keep the existing shell: full-height left sidebar (wordmark, property switcher in mono, nav with "Summary" as the new first and active item above Overview, Issues, Crawl health, Network, Search performance, Opportunities, Links, Research; data-freshness block pinned to the sidebar foot) and a slim top bar (page title "Site summary", a mono date-window chip "28d vs prior 28d", a "Print / share" outlined button).

Panels, in this order:
1. Freshness band (full width, slim): four source pills - Search Console, Crawl, Audit, URL inspection - each with a status dot, the age ("[n]d ago") and the window it covers in mono. A pill turns amber when a source is stale.
2. Verdict line (full width): one plain-English sentence in the sans face at 18px, built from the numbers below (e.g. "Organic clicks are [down] [figure]% on the prior 28 days; [figure] critical issues are open; [figure] pages changed since the last crawl."). Beside it three small status chips: trend direction, critical issues, changes since last crawl.
3. KPI strip of five tiles: Organic clicks (28d, delta vs prior, delta year-on-year, 16-month sparkline); Non-brand clicks (share of clicks, delta); Site health (score out of 100 with a step-line sparkline across past audits, and a one-line definition under it: "share of crawled pages with no critical or high issue"); Indexed (indexed of inspected URLs, as "[figure] of [figure] inspected"); AI readiness (score out of 100). KPI values in the sans face at 28px.
4. "Better or worse" row, 8+4: left card = monthly organic clicks over 16 months as thin bars with a dashed ghost line for the same month last year, plus small event markers on the x-axis for crawls and audits; right card = site health score per audit as a step line, with opened-vs-resolved issue counts per audit as tiny paired bars beneath it.
5. "What is broken" and "Do this first" row, 6+6: left = a compact table of issue areas (Indexation, Crawlability, On-page, Content, Structured data, Performance, Security) with columns pages affected (thin bar + mono count), critical and high count chips, and change since last audit ("+[n] new / -[n] resolved"); right = a numbered list of the five highest-impact actions, each row with the action title, pages affected, clicks at risk, an effort size chip (S/M/L/XL), an evidence label chip (D = deterministic, N = judgement) and a thin impact bar.
6. Three equal cards: "Changed since last crawl" (rows: pages now erroring, noindex added, canonical changed, title changed - each with a severity dot and mono count); "Ranking mix" (a 100% stacked bar per period - positions 1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21+ - this period vs prior); "Winners and losers" (top three pages gaining clicks, top three losing, middle-truncated URLs with mono deltas).
7. "AI search" card (full width, split in three): agent-readiness score with four category pass bars (Discoverability, Content, Bot access, Capabilities); AI crawler access as a row of chips per bot (allowed in green, blocked in red, not stated in grey); AI Overview presence as "[figure] of [figure] checked queries show an AI Overview, cited on [figure]", with the sample size visible. Include one muted slot that says "Answer share in chat assistants: not measured" - the design must show honestly what is not collected.
8. Data coverage footer (full width, slim): chips naming what the page is built on and what is missing - Search Console [figure] months, Crawl [figure] pages, URL inspection [figure] of [figure], Lab Core Web Vitals [figure] pages, Field Core Web Vitals not collected, Server logs not collected.

Rules: never two y-axes on one chart; sans face for all UI text, mono only for figures, URLs, axis ticks and micro-labels; tables never overflow their card (middle-truncate URLs); red only for genuine problems; no gradients, glow, illustrations or emoji. Every number is a placeholder in the form [figure]. Do not invent figures, domains or brand names.
